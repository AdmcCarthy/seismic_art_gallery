Changes made to the original data
- Only small derived extracts are included. The 3D SEG-Y volume and the interpretation
  files are NOT included.
- Rings: three circular sections read from the volume (nearest trace, no interpolation),
  amplitudes scaled to 8-bit integers over one clip value.
- Cities: windows of the volume around at most 3,500 picks of each of eight interpreted
  surfaces (every k-th pick), aligned by a matched filter, with summary statistics.
- Growth rings and flow: root-mean-square amplitude of such windows, and the apparent
  dip of one interpreted surface smoothed over 150 m.
- Trace shells: the analytic signal (envelope, phase, instantaneous frequency) of 48
  traces. Fault constellations: a per-pick discontinuity along one interpreted surface,
  with the interpreters' fault polygons for that surface as reference lines. One thread:
  an 80 ms slab of every trace, 8-bit.
- Surface names are read from the interpreters' file names; one abbreviation (BCU, Base
  Cretaceous Unconformity) is expanded from general knowledge, not from the files.
- The vertical domain (time) and quantity (amplitude) are stated by the publisher of
  this gallery, not by the files; polarity is unknown.
