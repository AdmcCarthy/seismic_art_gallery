# seismic_art gallery

**Seismic amplitude as art, from a model and from the Volve field.**

A gallery of stills, each opening the live D3 sketch it was taken from: rings, waveform
cities, growth rings, flow fields, trace shells, fault constellations, one thread, and the
Volve surveys of 2002 and 2010 drawn over each other as double exposure rings, unwinding
shells and an engraving of what changed. GitHub Pages serves it from this repo.

## What is shown
The front page (`index.html`) holds the chosen views, under a header band cut from the
Volve seismic itself with its interpreted surfaces (`header.html` shows the band on its
own, with its options). `sketches.html` lists every sketch
with its options, and `volve.html` the real-data runs. The model figures are synthetic:
generated by the `seismic_art` project from a convolutional model with a known truth, and
labelled so. The Volve figures are small extracts of the real surveys and their interpreted
surfaces, named from the interpreters' own files. Amplitudes are relative, and every
sketch states its domain, polarity and source in its caption; the stills carry the
attribution in their file comment.

## How this repo is made
It is a published copy of the `web/` folder of the private `seismic_art` project, written
by that project's `scripts/publish_gallery.py` after `scripts/render_gallery.py` has made
the stills. Edit the project, not this copy.

`vendor/d3.min.js` is D3 7.9.0 (ISC licence, `vendor/D3-LICENSE.txt`). `vendor/subscout/`
holds the colour tokens and base styles of [SubScout](https://github.com/AdmcCarthy/SubScout),
copied unchanged (MIT License with Acknowledgement; the colour method follows the Equinor
Design System palette generator, MIT).
