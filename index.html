Seismic art sketches

Data art from seismic amplitude. Every example here is synthetic, generated by seismic_art from a convolutional model (no real survey). Amplitudes are relative; each figure states its domain and polarity. Served from web/.

A · Seismic rings

A circle cut from the volume and wrapped into polar form: angle along the circle, radius = time. Callouts mark the clearest layers and their brightest trace.

image wiggle paper SVG view

B · Waveform cities

Waveforms along a thin-bed wedge, grouped by a self-organising map.

dark paper k-means named surfaces SVG view

C · Growth rings per horizon

An attribute round each site on five horizons, oldest outermost.

dark paper SVG view

D · Flow over a surface

Streamlines of steepest descent on a picked horizon, coloured by amplitude. Callouts mark structural highs, lows and saddles.

dark paper paper, updip SVG view

Volve, real data
