Seismic as art

Amplitude from a 3D survey, drawn as rings, cities, shells, constellations and one thread; and two surveys of the same field, eight years apart, laid over each other as one picture. Each still opens the live sketch, which carries its caption, domain, polarity and source.

The Volve field

Real data: small extracts of the Volve 3D surveys (2002 and 2010) and their interpreted surfaces, drawn with the same rules as the model. Amplitudes are relative; polarity is not stated by the data.

The model

Synthetic: a convolutional model with a thin-bed wedge, a fault, a fluid trend and noise, so every rule can be checked against a known truth before it meets real data.

Terms: Volve data sharing; changes and disclaimer in NOTICE.txt. Only small derived extracts are shown, never the volumes.

The synthetic figures are generated by seismic_art from a model; no real survey is behind them, and they say so.
